# Privacy Policy — Junior - Notes

**Last updated:** 10 September 2026

## The short version

Junior - Notes collects nothing. Your notes never leave your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no
crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

## Where your notes are stored

Every note you write is saved in a database file inside the app's own private
storage on your device. That storage is sandboxed by iOS: no other app can
read it.

Your notes are included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a local
encrypted backup) if you have backups enabled. That backup is handled entirely
by Apple under Apple's terms — we have no access to it and receive no copy.

## What we send over the network

Nothing. The app makes no network requests. It works with no internet
connection, permanently.

## Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not ask for, and cannot access, your
camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, calendar, health data or
notifications.

## Third parties

There are no third-party services, SDKs or analytics providers in the app. No
data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a note inside the app removes it. Deleted notes are recoverable for a
short window via Undo, and are permanently erased 30 days after deletion.

Deleting the app from your device permanently removes every note along with it.
Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing left to request or
erase afterwards.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects anything, this policy will be updated
before that version ships, and the change will be described here.
